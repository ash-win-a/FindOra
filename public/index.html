How Findora Works

Traditional campus lost-and-found boxes lead to privacy breaches and theft. Findora uses algorithmic matching and confidential ownership verification to safely return items.

1. Report an Item

Submit details such as category, location, date, and safe photos of lost or found belongings.

2. Intelligent Matching

Our explainable algorithm calculates category, title, color, location, and date similarity up to 100%.

3. Verified Claim

Submit confidential proof or answer verification questions that only the rightful owner knows.

4. Safe Handover

Once the finder confirms your proof, arrange a verified handover on campus and mark the item Returned.

Safe Returning Guidelines

Findora protects your personal contact details. Always prioritize personal safety and follow university policies when returning lost property.

Start Browsing
Meet in Public Places

Always arrange item handovers at well-lit campus areas like the Library desk or Security Office.

Verify Before Handing

Never hand over high-value electronics without matching serial numbers or passcode verification.

Guard Private Details

Do not publish PINs, wallet cash amounts, or government IDs openly in public description fields.

Update Item Status

Mark items as Returned after successful handover to keep our campus database accurate.
